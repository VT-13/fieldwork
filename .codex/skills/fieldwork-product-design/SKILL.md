description: Design or substantially change Fieldwork screens, navigation, visual components, or interaction patterns; maintain its design system and accessible student-focused experience.
---

Read [product design reference](references/design-system.md). The reference records the implemented Module 3 system, verified through browser critique and refinement. Reuse its warm paper/forest/serif/ruled-ledger language and shared primitives; update the reference when an implemented, rendered change alters the system.

Make opportunities, email intent, delivery state, and next actions clear. Distinguish practice messages, approved outreach, accepted sends, confirmed Sent receipts, and replies. Never present configured credentials as a healthy connection or imply a local Mac runs while asleep.

Design coherent navigation, reusable surfaces, tables, forms, charts, and responsive layouts. Specify type, color, spacing, borders, elevation, icons, motion, loading, empty and error states together. Use persistent labels, keyboard access, visible focus and sufficient contrast; status cannot depend on color alone. Use restrained microinteractions and honor reduced motion. Give dense tables deliberate scrolling and keep primary actions reachable on small screens.

Verify desktop and mobile in a browser; inspect screenshots, keyboard flows, and failures. Preserve the user's personal-first scope unless a later phase explicitly expands it. Keep implementation and provider billing details out of primary task flows unless they affect a decision.

Use the overview/prospect/campaign/review hierarchy in the reference. Put relevance before evidence in the mobile document order; keep email evidence adjacent on laptops. Review decisions precede the reading surface and must not obscure it. Do not add unverified performance visuals or a cosmetic campaign-resume control.
